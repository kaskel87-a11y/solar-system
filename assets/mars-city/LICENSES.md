# Mars city asset licenses (Solar System Explorer)

Every shipped asset here is **CC0-1.0**, either upstream or as our own work released under CC0. Nothing with unclear licensing was included, and no attribution is legally required. Courtesy credits are listed anyway.

Sources were downloaded by `tools/fetch.py` / `tools/fetch_tex.py`. Per-model upstream `LICENSE.txt` / `manifest.json` (Grab3D) are kept in `src/grab3d/<slug>/`, and download metadata is in `src/fetch_meta.json`. The phone/desktop GLBs are derived works: simplified, resized and recompressed, with the same license.
