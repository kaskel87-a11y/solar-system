# NPC Barks

Short lines for the radio card and the speech toasts, 65 characters max each. The prefix shows who says it; the game picks one at random. The existing station blurbs are in `audio7.js` STATIONS[].dj, and the lines below add to them. Off-world radio flavor lives in each `planets/*.md`.

- "You see that guy's eyes? Blue. Glowing. Dust, man."

- "Is that a dog in a helmet? Hey, buddy! Hey!"

- "Saltblood boys outside the Cantina again. Red tape, big guns."
- "Rook was asking about you. That's never good."

## Random street events (ideas, one per colony visit at most)
- **Mugging in progress:** a colonist is getting rolled by two thugs. Help (+rep, 40 cr) or ignore. "Help! They took my tank!"
- **Crashed hauler:** "Somebody help, he's pinned!" Free the driver (+30 cr), or loot the cargo (+1★).
- **Dust deal gone bad:** two dealers shooting it out. The winner drops dust.
- **Lost dog flyer:** "Have you seen this pug?" Frankie finds him. Reward: a bandana.
- **Drag challenge:** "Nice ride. Race you to the pad?" A 20-second sprint for 50 cr.
- **Protest at Meridian tower:** "Water not resin!" Guards turn up at 1★ if you join in.
