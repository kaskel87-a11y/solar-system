# v8 realistic look in the game (`window.SSX.look`)

This brings the look-dev v8 look (kaskel87-a11y/solar-system-lookdev) to planet surfaces. It is an add-on: new files plus one `<script>` hook in `index.html`, with **no core edits**. Space is untouched.

## How it hooks in
- `look/v8.js` wraps `__app.renderer.render`.
  - Only the surface draw (`__app.surface.scene/camera`) is intercepted. Every other render passes straight through, including inner renders (PMREM, shadow, composer).
- `SSX.registerSurfaceTick` tracks the current planet.
- `SSX.registerMenuItem` adds ☰ **✨ Graphics** (auto, low, med, high, ultra, or classic).
- Light values are only *scaled* while the frame draws, then put back exactly:
  - sun ×1.25 and hemi ×0.5 outdoors;
  - emissive ×(1 + 1.4·night).
  - So **SSX.time (#30)**, **Director's storm filter** and anything else that writes the lights keep ownership of them.
- Classic switches back to the v7 path at runtime: materials are unpatched, the env/composer is disposed, and shadows and pixel ratio are restored.

## What it does
- **Lighting**
  - A shadow-casting sun that follows the player or car, with texel snapping.
  - ACES tone mapping comes from the core.
  - IBL from a PMREM of a procedural sky built from the *live* sky uniforms, hemi ground and sun colour. It rebuilds when they change, so it follows the clock and the storm. No HDRI download.
- **Materials**, patched in place with `onBeforeCompile` and reversible:
  - Terrain gets regolith/rock PBR detail: slope rock, macro variation, two-scale anti-tiling on high, and triplanar rock on high/ultra.
  - It modulates the existing vertex-colour palette, so biomes and art direction stay intact.
  - Buildings and vehicles get dust and grime weathering, plus concrete or metal-panel micro detail on large plain surfaces.
- **Post** (high/ultra): HalfFloat composer → [GTAO on ultra] → bloom → OutputPass → grade (per planet and per interior; vignette, grain).
- **Night:** emissive and neon get a boost scaled by `SSX.time.nightness()`, or by the sun height when SSX.time isn't present. Bloom strength and threshold follow ART.md (day 0.45/1.2, night 0.8/1.0).

## Tiers

| tier | pixel ratio cap | shadows | IBL | post | detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | 1.25 | none | metals only | none (CSS vignette) | single-scale, 1-octave noise |
| **med** (phone default) | 1.5 | 1 cascade, 1024, PCF, updated every 3rd frame, ±28 m | metals only (hemi carries the fill) | **none**: no bloom, no full-screen passes, CSS vignette | single-scale, 1-octave noise, no triplanar |
| high (desktop default) | 2 | 2048 PCFSoft, ±42 m | all | bloom ½-res, grade, MSAA 4 | two-scale + triplanar |
| ultra | 2 | 4096 PCFSoft, ±48 m | all | + GTAO, bloom ¾-res | two-scale + triplanar |

- **Dynamic resolution** runs on every v8 tier:
  - It keeps an EMA of frame time. Over 18.5 ms it drops the pixel ratio 0.125 per step, down to a floor of about 0.75–1.0.
  - When it holds about 16.7 ms it probes back up one step. A failed probe doubles the wait before the next one, up to 30 s. vsync hides headroom, so probing is the only way to find it.
  - The composer is resized, not rebuilt.
- **The governor** steps an *auto* tier down only after dynamic resolution is already at its floor and fps stays under 45 for two 4 s windows.
- **Pick order:** `?look=`/`?tier=` → saved choice (`localStorage ssx.v8.v1`) → device class (phone → med, desktop → high).
- **Debug/bench switches:**
  - `?v8gov=0` and `?v8drs=0`, or `localStorage ssx.v8.nogov` / `ssx.v8.nodrs` = `'1'`;
  - `?v8nopost`, `?v8noshadow`, `?v8nodetail`, `?v8noenv`.

## Compatibility
- **SSX.time (#30):** this is feature-detected, with no hard dependency.
  - The sun, ambient and sky stay SSX.time's, and v8 only scales them per frame.
  - The env rebuilds as the sky changes.
  - Neon glows at night through the emissive boost (and bloom on high+).
- **Director:** its storm filter writes the sky, fog and lights, so it's picked up the same way.
- **Jeff's art kit (main: `world.setMaterialSlot`, `MATERIAL_ROLES`, `userData.v8`):**
  - v8 patches whatever material sits in a slot, in place, so swapping slots still works.
  - It skips skinned meshes, `dress:*` objects, `userData.weathered`, and `userData.noV8`.
  - Use `SSX.look.skip(obj)` to opt hand-made art out.

## API
`SSX.look` = `{ version, tier(), auto, tiers, TIERS, set(name|'auto'), onChange(fn), skip(obj), env(), nightness(), state() }`
